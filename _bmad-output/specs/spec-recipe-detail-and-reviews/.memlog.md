- (direction) Headless express distillation for the recipe-detail-and-reviews scope; preserve unresolved R5 detail as open questions.
- (decision by user) Reviews mean ratings imported from the recipe dataset, not user-submitted reviews.
- (capability) CAP-1: A cook can open a recipe from search results and inspect its full recipe details; success means the detail view identifies the selected recipe and presents the agreed detail fields without inventing unavailable values.
- (capability) CAP-2: A cook can view ratings imported from the source dataset for a recipe; success means displayed rating information is sourced from imported data and no rating submission is offered.
- (constraint) Recipe detail and rating content comes from the existing recipe dataset, including its ratings file; no runtime external API or LLM is introduced.
- (constraint) Do not add accounts, logins, user-submitted recipes, or user-submitted reviews; do not show or store recipe contributor identities or expose rater identities.
- (assumption) The ratings file referenced by the PRD is the source for review-like information; this scope does not create new ratings.
- (question) Which fields constitute full recipe details in the detail view, given the PRD names recipe, time, tags, and ingredients but does not specify a complete detail payload?
- (question) Should imported rating data be presented as an aggregate, individual rating records, or another summary?
- (decision) Adopt docs/data-model.md as a companion because its Recipe and Rating model is needed to interpret the existing R5 data contract.
- (decision) CAP-1 and CAP-2 define the independently visible R5 outcomes; user authentication, user-generated content, and personalized ranking remain out of scope.
- (event) Coherence validation PASS: both capabilities have WHAT intents and success criteria; constraints alter scope; non-goals are explicit; success is demonstrable; unresolved field and ratings-presentation details are open questions; CAP-1 and CAP-2 are unique; prose is lean.
- (event) Preservation validation PASS: R5 recipe details and dataset ratings, no accounts or user-submitted reviews, contributor/rater identity protections, dataset sourcing, and the adopted Recipe/Rating model are retained. Unrelated releases, deployment, and general project requirements were excluded as outside this spec's scope.
